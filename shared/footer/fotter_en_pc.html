Frequently Asked Questions

Q.

Why is device usage restricted for latecomers?

A.
Setting up devices in a dark auditorium
can disturb other audience members.

However, for shows with an intermission,
you will be guided to a seat with set up subtitles before Act 2 begins.

 ※ No same-day refunds will be given due to late entry, so please arrive with plenty of time. 
Q.

Are actors' ad-libs translated in real-time?

A.

Unexpected ad-libs on the day of the show are difficult to handle immediately.

However, UNISTEP continuously updates translations to maintain top quality so audiences don't miss the context and emotion.

How to use

How to Use UNISTEP

Here is the process from booking to the start of the show.



1. Service Booking

After completing payment, a 'Ticket Voucher' will be sent to your email within 1 hour.

Capturing the voucher in advance speeds up on-site verification.


2. Arrive at the Theater Day of Show

Please arrive at least 30 minutes before the show to receive your ticket and set up your device.


3. Pick up Physical Ticket

Present your voucher at the box office and receive your paper ticket.


4. Seating & Setup 15 min prior req.

Once seated, UNISTEP staff 'uni' will assist you with device angle adjustment and setup.


5. Enjoy the Show

All setup is complete.
Fully focus on the warmth of the stage!
