Frequently Asked Questions

Q.

Why is device usage restricted for latecomers?

A.
Setting up devices in a dark auditorium
can disturb other audience members.

However, for shows with an intermission,
you will be guided to a seat with set up subtitles before Act 2 begins.

※ No same-day refunds will be given due to late entry, so please arrive with plenty of time.
Q.

Are actors' ad-libs translated in real-time?

A.

Unexpected ad-libs on the day of the show are difficult to handle immediately.

However, UNISTEP continuously updates translations so audiences don't miss the context.

How to use

How to Use UNISTEP

Here is the process from booking to the show.



1. Service Booking

A 'Ticket Voucher' will be sent to your email.
Please capture it in advance.


2. Arrive at Theater Day of Show

Please arrive at least
30 minutes before the show starts.


3. Pick up Ticket

Present your voucher at the box office
and receive your paper ticket.


4. Seating & Setup 15 min prior

Staff 'uni' will assist you with
device adjustment and setup.


5. Enjoy the Show

All setup is complete.
Fully focus on the stage!
